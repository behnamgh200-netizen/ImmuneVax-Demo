Integrated ImmuneVax scientific demo

From structure inspection to population-level immune-model validation.

This is one project with two connected scientific layers. The first performs real local calculations on an uploaded PDB and shows a deliberately simple APC-facing educational heuristic. The second loads a privacy-safe export from the private mechanistic core and shows human-data calibration, participant holdout, and external cross-study validation without exposing private parameters or raw records.
