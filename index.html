Demo boundary

This branch is intentionally non-operational. It uses synthetic records and pre-scripted interactions to demonstrate software architecture and research workflow. It does not query IEDB, ImmPort, AlphaFold, FoldX, PhysiCell, or any other live scientific service, and it does not produce a real vaccine recommendation.

Research concept

One workspace, multiple evidence layers

ImmuneVax is designed as an orchestration and scientific-accountability layer: scientific records enter with provenance, external tools remain isolated behind adapters, uncertainty is explicit, and a human reviewer remains in control.

4 Evidence layers

Curated source, model output, simulation output, human decision.

Research workflow

From scientific evidence to a reviewable decision

The demo emphasizes the architecture and accountability of the workflow rather than reproducing the private scientific implementation.

01

Scientific inputs

Versioned source metadata, literature references, and curated immune evidence.

Planned: IEDB · ImmPort · UniProt · PDB · NCBI
02

Prediction adapters

Isolated interfaces for MHC, structural, and future AI-assisted prediction services.

Planned: AlphaFold · FoldX · specialized predictors
03

Evidence synthesis

Comparable candidate records with source-level provenance and model context.

No hidden aggregation without traceability
04

Immune simulation

A simplified internal model can later delegate to an advanced cellular engine.

Planned external adapter: PhysiCell
05

Uncertainty

Missing evidence, model disagreement, scope limits, and confidence are explicit.

Uncertainty is part of the record
06

Human review

A researcher must accept, revise, or reject the interpretation with rationale.

Automatic final approval: disabled
