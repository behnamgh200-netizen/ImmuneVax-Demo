Integrated research-pipeline demo

Sequence, structure, evidence, model adapters, and a toy immune-response simulation in one traceable workflow.

This public demo performs local parsing and descriptive analysis, maps imported evidence, reads model-result files, and runs an explicitly illustrative normalized simulation. It does not design vaccines or claim clinical efficacy.
